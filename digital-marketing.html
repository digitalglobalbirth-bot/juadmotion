A few quick answers

Before we get started.

Do you guarantee a specific number of leads or sales?
No. Outcomes depend on the offer, audience, budget, timing and other factors. We agree on measurable goals and report transparently without promising results we cannot control.
Is advertising spend included in your fee?
No. Platform ad spend is paid separately by the client. The management scope and budget are agreed before campaigns begin.
Can you work with our existing team?
Yes. We can scope creative, planning or campaign support to complement your internal team or other partners.
