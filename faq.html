Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

Find quick answers about how projects are scoped, reviewed and delivered. If your question isn’t here, send us a note.

How long does a project take?
Turnaround varies with the deliverables, footage or campaign complexity, and feedback timing. We’ll confirm a project timeline in writing before work begins.
How many revisions are included?
Revision rounds depend on the chosen package or agreed scope. The proposal will state the number of rounds and how to submit feedback.
How does the payment process work?
Payment milestones and accepted methods are listed in your proposal or invoice. A project starts once the scope and initial payment terms are approved.
Which software do you use?
Depending on the brief, we work with tools including Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop, Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads.
Will I receive the editable source files?
Final exports are included as specified in your scope. Editable project files can be discussed in advance and may require an additional handoff fee.
Do you guarantee campaign results?
No. Performance depends on many factors outside a creative partner’s control. We can agree on goals, track relevant indicators and make informed adjustments without promising a specific result.
Is advertising spend part of the service fee?
No. Media spend is separate and paid directly to the platform by the client. We agree any management fee and campaign budget before launch.
What do I need to provide to get started?
A short brief, your objectives, intended audience, relevant brand assets, references and timing are a useful starting point. We’ll follow up if we need anything else.
